## Text formatting

- **Bold text** carries the weight of a line.

- *Italic text* adds a lighter emphasis.

- <u>Underlined text</u> marks a term.

- <span data-stormpad-color="blue">Colored text</span> uses a StormPad palette color.

- <span data-stormpad-highlight="yellow">Highlighted text</span> calls out a passage.

- [A link](https://mattferre95.github.io/StormPad/) opens a page in your browser.

## Images and files

Hover the left edge of any block and click the Add Block button, then choose Image or File. The Format menu offers the same choices under Block Type. StormPad copies what you add into your own Attachments folder, so the note keeps working offline.

## Projects and organization

- Projects work like folders for related notes

- Unfiled holds every note that is not in a Project

- Drag a note between Projects in the sidebar

- Pin a note to keep it at the top of the list

- Search looks across every note you have written

## Sharing

Share a note as a readable TXT file through native macOS services such as AirDrop, Messages, and Mail.

After you AirDrop the TXT file to an iPhone, you can open it there or share it into Apple Notes yourself. That is a manual transfer, not automatic Apple Notes synchronization.

## Local and private

Your notes stay on your Mac, in this folder:
